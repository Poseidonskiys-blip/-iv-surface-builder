Implied Volatility Surface Builder

Invert Black–Scholes on every option price to see the volatility the market is really quoting. A flat surface is what the model assumes. What you see is the smile and skew.

Sample data is synthetic: prices are generated from a skewed vol model, then inverted. For real data, export a chain (for example with Python’s yfinance: Ticker(sym).option_chain(date)), use the mid of bid and ask, and paste it here. Per strike, the out-of-the-money option is used, since it is the most liquid.
